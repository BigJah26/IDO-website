The IDSC

The International Dueling Supreme Court oversees clan servers and rules on disputes. Clan leaders may be summoned to present their case.

Rules

The basics. The full rulebook lives in the Discord.

  1. Respect everyone. No harassment, hate speech, or targeting other members or clans.
  2. No exploits or cheating. Any use of exploits or third-party tools means disqualification.
  3. Show up on time. Teams not ready at match time may forfeit.
  4. Staff and IDSC decisions are final. Disputes go through the proper channels.
  5. Follow Roblox and Discord terms. Always.
