The IDSC

The International Dueling Supreme Court is IDO's community-led branch that oversees clan servers and settles disputes.

Oversight

Monitors clan servers to make sure they follow IDO standards.

Hearings

Clan leaders can be summoned to present their case before the court.

Rulings

Decisions are final and help keep the competitive scene fair for everyone.

Rules

The basics. The full rulebook lives in the Discord.

  1. Respect everyone. No harassment, hate speech, or targeting other members or clans.
  2. No exploits or cheating. Any use of exploits or third-party tools means disqualification.
  3. Show up on time. Teams not ready at match time may forfeit.
  4. Staff and IDSC decisions are final. Disputes go through the proper channels.
  5. Follow Roblox and Discord terms. Always.

Prove yourself in the arena

Whether you lead a clan or you're just getting started, this is where reputations are made.
